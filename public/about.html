Pattern Fit

About Pattern Fit

Pattern Fit is a free browser-based tool for creating transparent Grid, Dots, and Stripes patterns.

Adjust spacing, size, thickness, color, layout, angle, and canvas dimensions, then download the result as a transparent PNG.

It is designed for use with Canva, Figma, PowerPoint, Google Slides, and other design tools.

Free to use. No sign-up required.
